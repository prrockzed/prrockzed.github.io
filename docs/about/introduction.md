# Introduction

My core focus is backend development, with strong expertise in Java, Python, PostgreSQL, distributed systems, and cloud-native tooling. I gravitate toward problems that demand both performance and reliability — systems that work correctly at scale, under real-world conditions.

Outside of engineering, I'm drawn to **Chess**, **Tennis**, and **Finance** — Chess for its depth and precision, Tennis for the discipline and competition it demands, and Finance for how it connects systems thinking to the real world.

This site is where I document my work, projects, and things I pick up along the way.
